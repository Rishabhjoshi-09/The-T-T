<h1>The T T</h1>
The T T is a fast-paced table tennis game you can play against a friend on the same keyboard or against the computer on Easy, Medium, Hard. A coin toss decides who serves first, and the first to 10 points wins. The ball gets faster as the match goes on, and hitting it with the center of your racket sends it flying. Keep returning shots to build your streak and watch and flame grow. When either side reaches match point, the screen glows red and the music kicks up.
